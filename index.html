Peak-Event Intraday Performance Tracker

Hourly performance against plan for six anonymised brands across two sales channels. Built to show where a revenue target is at risk while there is still time to act.

Business question

Which brands will miss their peak-day target, by how much, and what should account owners do in the next hours?

Approach

Hourly checkpoints per brand and channel are consolidated into cumulative performance, compared with an expected-share curve, and converted into pace, forecast at close and required run-rate. Rule-based flags separate funnel, engagement and paid-media issues.

Decision support

Every hour the view ranks brands by variance to target and lists the interventions the rules would trigger, so effort can be reallocated before the day closes.
